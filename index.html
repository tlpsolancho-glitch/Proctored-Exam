Data Privacy Consent

This exam is proctored. By continuing you agree that:

  • Your camera and microphone must stay on for the whole exam.
  • Your webcam feed is analyzed on your device to detect your face and gaze direction. The video is not recorded or streamed.
  • When an event is flagged (face missing, multiple faces, looking away for several seconds, leaving the exam page, loud sound), a single still image and the event details are saved for your instructor to review.
  • Flags are only indicators. A person, not the system, decides if any action is needed.
  • Data is kept only as long as needed for grading and review, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
